Privacy

A quiet space, with clear boundaries

The anonymous-question form is optional. AUCOSMOS does not ask for your name, email address, account, or contact details.

What is collected

The form collects the question you choose to submit. Please do not include names, locations, or other details that could identify you or someone else.

How the form works

Questions are processed by Formspree, an external form service, and delivered to a designated AUCOSMOS inbox. The Formspree submission archive is disabled, so questions are not retained in its submission dashboard. Formspree may still process technical information needed to deliver the form, prevent abuse, and maintain security. You can read Formspree’s privacy policy.

How questions may be used

A submitted question may inspire a future Moment of Tafakkur or another AUCOSMOS reflection. Before any question is quoted or shared, identifying details will be removed. Submission does not guarantee publication or a personal response.

Retention

The inbox copy will be deleted after it is no longer needed. A de-identified question may be retained as a private editorial note when it is being considered for a future reflection. Formspree may retain limited technical logs under its own security and legal obligations.

Please remember

This form is not intended for urgent help, private counselling, or religious rulings. Do not submit information that you would be unsafe or uncomfortable sharing through an online form.
